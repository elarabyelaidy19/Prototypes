Service Overview

The Inheritance Calculator implements Islamic inheritance law (علم الفرائض) as a pure Ruby service pipeline. It takes a deceased person's gender, estate amount, and list of heirs — then computes each heir's legally mandated share according to Quranic fixed shares (فرض) and residuary rules (تعصيب), handling blocking (حجب), proportional reduction (عول), and surplus redistribution (رد).

Public API: Inheritance::Calculator.new(deceased_gender:, estate_amount:, heirs:).calculate → returns a Result struct consumed by the controller and view.

Share Distribution Logic توزيع الأنصبة

Shares are either fixed (فرض) — Quranic fractions — or residuary (تعصيب) — what remains after fixed shares.

Fixed Shares فرض

Quranic fractions assigned by law based on heir type, count, and presence of other heirs.

Residuary تعصيب

Heirs who receive the remainder after fixed shares. Males receive 2x female weight.

Weight system: Each male heir gets weight=2, female gets weight=1. Remainder is split proportionally by count * weight per heir type. E.g., 2 sons + 1 daughter: sons share = 4/5 of remainder, daughter = 1/5.

Dynamic blocking: When full sisters become residuary heirs with daughters (عصبة مع الغير), they additionally block paternal siblings mid-computation. The enforce_blocking! safety net then scrubs any stale shares.
Special Cases
عول Awl

When total fixed shares exceed 1, all shares are proportionally reduced.

Example: Wife (1/4) + Mother (1/6) + 2 Full Sisters (2/3) = 13/12 > 1. Each share shrinks by the same ratio.

رد Radd

When total fixed shares fall short of 1 with no residuary heirs, the surplus is redistributed to non-spouse fixed heirs.

Spouses never receive Radd. Surplus goes proportionally to other fixed-share heirs.

العمريتان

When only spouse + both parents exist: mother gets 1/3 of the remainder (not 1/3 of the whole estate).

Named after the ruling of Umar ibn al-Khattab. Father takes whatever is left as residuary.
